subroutine, public ccpr_seasalt_common_mod::weibulldistribution ( real(fp), intent(inout)  gweibull,
logical, intent(in)  weibullflag,
real(fp), intent(in)  wm,
integer, intent(out)  rc 
)

Calculate the weibull distribution for 10m wind speed (u10, v10)

The Weibull distribution correction ends up being a multiplicative constant (g) times our present source function (see Eq. 12 in Fan & Toon, 2011 and notes for (9/22/11). This constant is derived from the incomplete and complete forms of the gamma function, hence the utilities pasted below. The Weibull function and shape parameters (k, c) assumed are from Justus 1978.

Parameters
[in,out]gweibullMultiplicative constant
[in]weibullFlagFlag for weibull correction
[in]wm10m wind speed
[out]RCReturn Code
